Key Takeaway

We believe in radical clarity: You own 100% of all finalized deliverables upon milestone payment. Sprints are executed in synchronized agile phases with clear acceptance criteria.

1. Engagement Scope & Statement of Work (SOW)

These Terms & Conditions ("Terms") govern all digital marketing, software development, AI model deployment, video production, and branding services provided by BrandBhai Studio Private Limited ("BrandBhai") to the client named in any executed Proposal, Statement of Work (SOW), or Service Agreement ("Client").

Each SOW defines the specific deliverables, timelines, milestone schedules, and financial compensation for the selected discipline(s).

2. Intellectual Property Rights & Asset Vault

Full Ownership Transfer: Subject to full payment of all agreed milestone fees, BrandBhai assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to the custom deliverables created exclusively for Client (including custom code repositories, master video edits, brand vector marks, and fine-tuned AI prompts).

Pre-Existing Frameworks: BrandBhai retains ownership of its proprietary pre-existing utility libraries, baseline shader templates, and internal development tooling used to construct deliverables. Client receives a perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide license to utilize these incorporated tools as part of their final product.

3. Fees, Milestone Invoicing & Ad Spend

  • Service Fees: Milestone payments are invoiced according to the schedule specified in the SOW (typically 50% upon project kickoff, 50% upon final go-live deployment).
  • Third-Party Ad Spend: All direct ad spend (Meta Ads, Google Ads, YouTube Ads) is paid directly by Client to the advertising networks via Client’s billing methods. BrandBhai does not hold or markup ad spend.
  • Taxes: All quoted fees are exclusive of applicable Indian Goods and Services Tax (GST) or international withholding taxes.

4. Client Materials & Timely Approvals

To maintain our 10-day sprint velocity, Client agrees to provide necessary brand assets, product catalog data, API credentials, and feedback in a timely manner. Delays in providing required feedback will adjust downstream milestone delivery dates proportionately.

5. Warranties & Limitation of Liability

BrandBhai warrants that all custom code and creative deliverables are crafted with professional industry diligence, free of malicious code, and compliant with current browser standards.

Except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct, BrandBhai's total aggregate liability arising under any SOW shall be strictly limited to the total fees paid by Client to BrandBhai under that specific SOW in the preceding 3 months.

6. Term & Cancellation

Either party may terminate a monthly recurring retainer upon providing thirty (30) days prior written notice. Fixed-price sprint projects may be terminated by mutual written consent, subject to payment for all work completed up to the termination date.

7. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These Terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in New Delhi, India.
