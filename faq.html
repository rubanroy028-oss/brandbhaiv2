Everything you need to know before partnering with us.

Clear, transparent answers about our sprint timelines, deliverables, technical stack, pricing frameworks, and how our 5 disciplines operate as one synchronized unit.

01 Working Model & Sprints

How does the 10-day launch sprint work?

From the day your discovery blueprint is finalized, our parallel squads execute simultaneously across filming, LLM fine-tuning, landing page development, and ad setup. By Day 10, your campaigns, AI bot, and conversion architecture go live in unison.

Can we hire BrandBhai for individual disciplines or only full syndicates?

You can engage us either for standalone disciplines (e.g., just deploying a 24/7 WhatsApp AI lead engine, or commissioning a 4K cinema brand film) or as an end-to-end growth syndicate where all five disciplines compound together.

Who will be our primary point of contact?

Every client is assigned a dedicated Growth Partner and Technical Lead who coordinate all squad disciplines. You receive a private Slack/WhatsApp channel with real-time updates and weekly executive reviews.

02 AI Automation & WhatsApp Bots

Is the WhatsApp Bot built on official Meta Cloud APIs?

Yes, 100%. We only deploy on the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API with verified business manager accounts. This guarantees 0% risk of account bans, high-volume throughput (10,000+ msgs/minute), and official Green-Tick verification readiness.

How does the AI bot prevent hallucinations or inaccurate pricing?

We implement strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) vector architectures with temperature bounding and deterministic fallback policies. If a prospect asks a query outside the verified knowledge boundary, the bot immediately triggers human takeover rather than guessing.

Which CRMs and calendars does the bot integrate with?

We natively support HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho CRM, LeadSquared, Pipedrive, Google Sheets, Airtable, Calendly, Google Calendar, Cal.com, and custom webhook REST APIs.

03 Performance Ads & Media Buying

What is the minimum ad spend required to work with you?

We typically partner with brands spending at least ₹1.5L ($2,000 USD) per month on paid media up to multi-crore monthly scaling budgets. This ensures sufficient statistical significance for rapid creative hook testing.

Do you provide the video ad creative or do we need to supply it?

We produce 100% of the creative in-house. Our studio handles filming, UGC creator coordination, 4K cinema shooting, motion graphics, and rapid A/B hook variations delivered weekly.

How do you handle iOS 14.5+ attribution and tracking loss?

We deploy server-side Conversions API (CAPI) on custom cloud containers, Google Tag Manager server setups, and multi-touch attribution dashboards (Triple Whale / Northbeam) to eliminate tracking blind spots.

04 4K Cinema, Web & Brand Strategy

What camera systems and gear do you use for video shoots?

Our productions shoot on ARRI Alexa Mini LF and RED V-Raptor 8K cinema cameras with Cooke/Atlas anamorphic prime lenses, licensed high-speed 4K FPV drones, and dedicated DaVinci Resolve color grading suites.

What tech stack do you use for bespoke websites?

We build ultra-fast, zero-bloat web flagships using clean semantic HTML5, Vanilla CSS tokens, Lenis smooth scroll, Three.js / WebGL, GSAP, and headless Shopify Plus / Next.js architectures scoring 99/100 on Google Lighthouse.

Do we own all intellectual property and raw assets?

Yes. Upon completion of milestone payments, you receive 100% unrestricted intellectual property ownership of all vector source files, codebase repositories, master video ProRes exports, brand guidelines, and AI models.

Have a specific question not covered here?

Speak directly with our studio founders. We respond to every inquiry within 2 business hours.
