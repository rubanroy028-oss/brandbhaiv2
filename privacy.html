Summary in Plain English

We respect your privacy completely. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Any corporate documents or customer catalogs you provide for AI model fine-tuning are stored in private, isolated vector databases and will never be used to train public models.

1. Overview & Commitment

BrandBhai Studio ("BrandBhai", "we", "us", or "our") operates the website brandbhai.in and delivers bespoke services across AI automation, performance marketing, cinema production, and digital architecture.

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, and safeguard information provided by visitors, prospective clients, and enterprise partners in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 (India), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU/UK), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide our services and communicate effectively:

  • Contact Information: Name, work email address, company name, phone number, and WhatsApp handle submitted via our discovery forms or booking calendars.
  • Project & Business Intelligence: Current monthly ad spend, business category, website URLs, and technical requirements shared during consultations.
  • Technical Telemetry: IP addresses, browser user agent, device screen resolutions, and referral URLs collected automatically for performance optimization.

3. AI Model Training & Sovereign Vector Policy

For clients engaging our 24/7 AI WhatsApp Automation & Lead Systems:

  • Private Vector Enclaves: Proprietary product catalogs, PDFs, price sheets, and internal knowledge bases are converted into isolated RAG embeddings hosted on dedicated vector cloud tenants.
  • Zero Public Model Training: Your confidential corporate data and chat transcripts are strictly isolated and never fed into public LLM training datasets (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Meta).
  • End-to-End Encryption: Customer WhatsApp conversations passing through the official Meta Cloud API are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256 standards.

4. Tracking Technologies & Server-Side CAPI

Our website uses cookies and server-to-server analytics (including Meta Conversions API and Google Analytics 4) to analyze user engagement and verify ad campaign attribution. You have full granular control over cookie preferences via our interactive Cookie Policy page.

5. Enterprise Security & Data Retention

We implement bank-grade administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. Client credentials, API keys, and CRM access tokens are stored in encrypted secret vaults with multi-factor authentication (MFA) and least-privilege role-based access control (RBAC).

6. Your Rights & Access Controls

Regardless of your geographic location, you retain full rights over your data, including:

  • The right to request a complete export of all personal data held by BrandBhai.
  • The right to request immediate erasure of your contact information and CRM records.
  • The right to revoke consent for promotional communications at any time with one click.

7. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

If you have questions, data deletion requests, or security inquiries, please contact our designated Data Protection Officer:

Email: privacy@brandbhai.in / hello@brandbhai.in
