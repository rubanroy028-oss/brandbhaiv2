Cookie Summary

We use essential cookies to remember your theme preference (Dark/Light mode) and maintain smooth navigation. We only activate analytical or marketing pixels if you interact with ad funnels.

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. In addition to HTTP cookies, modern web applications utilize browser localStorage and sessionStorage to store UI preferences (such as your chosen theme or animation settings) without making repeated network calls.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary: Essential for the website to function properly (e.g., maintaining theme state, security tokens, anti-spam honeypot checks).
  • Performance & Analytics: Anonymous telemetry measuring page load times, WebGL canvas frame rates, and aggregate engagement to improve user experience.
  • Marketing & Attribution: Cookies that attribute conversions from Meta Ads or Google Search to optimize ad efficiency without profiling sensitive private data.

4. Server-Side Conversions API (CAPI)

To protect user privacy against third-party cross-site tracking, BrandBhai utilizes direct server-to-server Conversions API (CAPI) endpoints. This architecture avoids intrusive third-party scripts on the client device while ensuring verified attribution.

5. Managing Your Preferences

You can modify your cookie settings at any time via your browser preferences or by clearing your browser cache. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to browse our website or schedule discovery calls with our studio.
